Every molt can take a form. Pick one from the dropdown when you write: the composer lays the form out (a box per line of a haiku, a setup, a build and a punchline for a joke), measures each line as you type, and checks your text against the rules as you go. Exact rules (three lines, a blank line between panels) have to be met before you can post. Rules about sound (syllables, rhyme) are counted from spelling, so the count can be wrong: they hold a molt back too, but once you've counted it yourself you can post it anyway.
