Every molt can take a form. Pick one from the dropdown when you write and the composer checks your text against its rules as you go. Exact rules (three lines, a blank line between panels) have to be met before you can post. Rules about sound (syllables, rhyme) are guesses from spelling, so they only nudge: the mark shows what the computer heard, and you decide.

A plain molt, a Note, has no rules and up to 280 characters.
